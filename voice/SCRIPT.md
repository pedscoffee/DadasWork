# Dada's Work — Voice Script

## How to record

- **What you need:** Voice Memos on your phone, a quiet room, the phone about a foot away. Keep the same spot the whole time.
- **Each page:** 👏 **clap once**, say the page number ("Page five"), pause, then read the lines.
- **After every line:** pause about **one second** (a slow "one-Mississippi"). The pauses are how the lines get cut apart.
- **Messed up?** Just 👏 clap and start that page over. The last complete take of each page is the one that's kept.
- **Lines ending in "…"** are pieces that get glued to a word ("Find the word …" + "cat"). Say them like the middle of a sentence, without trailing off.
- **Single words:** clear and friendly, like you're answering a question.
- **Letter sounds:** short and clean ("buh", not "BUHHH"); hold the hissy ones (sss, mmm, fff) for about a second.
- **In chunks is fine.** Pages 1–8 (cheers, hints, letters, sounds, numbers) make the biggest difference, so they're a good first sitting. Any lines that aren't recorded yet keep using the device voice.
- **When you're done,** send me the recording(s). About 650 lines, roughly 30–40 minutes in total.
